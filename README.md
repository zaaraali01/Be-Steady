# Be Steady

A results companion for Bodywise customers, built for the Mosaic Fellowship Builder Round.

**Live app:** https://zaaraali01.github.io/be-steady/

## The problem

I spoke to five Bodywise customers. The two who keep buying could see a result. The other three either drifted off mid-routine or finished a full course, saw nothing, and left. Nobody told them what was normal or checked in.

> Bodywise customers have no way to tell whether a product is working. Without clear expectations, a complete routine or visible progress, they drift away mid-course or finish disappointed.

## What Be Steady does

- **Today:** the week you're on, when changes usually start, and your routine, including paired steps people skip (moisturiser after an AHA scrub, sunscreen with acids).
- **Timeline:** what's normal each week, built from Bodywise's own product pages and dermatology studies.
- **Photos:** weekly progress photos side by side, so slow change becomes visible.
- **Check-in:** a weekly "better, no change, worse" question. No change past the expected week routes the customer to Bodywise's free doctor consult with a summary ready to share.

## Notes

- One self-contained HTML file. No build step, no server.
- Data stays in the viewer's browser.
- A concept, not an official Bodywise product. Timelines are general guidance, not medical advice.
