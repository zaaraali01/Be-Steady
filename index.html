The research

Five customers. The two who keep buying could see a result. The other three couldn't, and nobody checked in.

Shabia stopped at six weeks. Junaid left two products half used on his shelf. Zaara finished a full 90 days, saw nothing, and now won't recommend the brand. Each of them reached a point where they needed someone to say what was normal, and nobody did.

Problem statement

Bodywise customers have no way to tell whether a product is working. Without clear expectations, a complete routine or visible progress, they drift away mid-course or finish disappointed, and Bodywise keeps only the customers who happen to see results on their own.

What I heard, and what Be Steady does about it

Why weekly check-ins, not daily reminders

95%of hair-treatment users who saw no results stopped, against 69% of those who improved (2023 study, 400 people).
74% vs 32%adherence when patients rated their own progress each week. Daily text reminders made no difference.
The gapBodywise's app has habit trackers and free doctor consults. It has no week-by-week results timeline and no progress photos.
